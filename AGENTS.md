# Mathematical Research Instructions

## Research Scope

This repository is used for research in mathematical analysis, partial differential equations, spectral theory, and hydrodynamic stability.

## Mathematical Rigor

1. Prioritize rigorous mathematical arguments over numerical experiments.
2. Clearly state all assumptions before proving a theorem.
3. Explain each nontrivial step in a proof.
4. Explicitly identify dependencies of constants in estimates.
5. Distinguish formal calculations from rigorous proofs.
6. Do not claim a theorem is proved when important steps remain unjustified.

## Mathematical Notation

1. Use LaTeX for mathematical expressions.
2. Preserve notation and definitions from the original problem.
3. Do not change mathematical symbols without explanation.
4. State the functional spaces, boundary conditions, and parameter ranges when relevant.

## Symbolic and Numerical Verification

1. Use SymPy for symbolic calculations when appropriate.
2. Use SciPy or mpmath for numerical verification.
3. Clearly distinguish numerical evidence from mathematical proof.
4. Check limiting cases and possible singularities.

## Literature

1. Never invent mathematical references.
2. Verify bibliographic information when possible.
3. Clearly identify unverified references.
4. Distinguish established mathematical results from conjectures.

## Research Output

For mathematical research tasks, organize results into:

1. Problem and assumptions.
2. Main result.
3. Detailed proof or derivation.
4. Verification and consistency checks.
5. Remaining gaps or open questions.
